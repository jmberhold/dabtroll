STATUS RULES

Choose exactly one status:

- complete: The required success outcome is established by a clear visible state or consistent temporal evidence. Brief occlusion does not prevent completion when the surrounding observations establish the required outcome. Occlusion, disappearance, or expected motion alone does not establish completion. Once the required outcome has been clearly demonstrated, do not demand another confirmation merely because a later view is obscured.

- running: The required outcome remains unmet, or the available evidence cannot distinguish completion from partial execution. Do not return running solely because fingers or the target are temporarily hidden when the visible sequence otherwise establishes completion.

- failure: Clear evidence establishes an unrecoverable failure or a precondition violation explicitly defined as terminal. Lack of visible completion alone is not failure. An empty hand after release or an object becoming hidden inside its destination is not by itself failure.

ACTION EXAMPLES

- Grasp and lift: Establish control of the target through a clear grip or carried motion demonstrating attachment to the hand. Contact, proximity, visual overlap, or motion consistent with pushing or sliding does not establish a grasp. When lifting is required, confirm the target moves clear of its supporting surface. Visible fingers are not required when the motion establishes attachment and lift. Do not add a lifting requirement to a grasp-only criterion.

- Transport: Confirm the target object itself reaches the position or region specified by this node's success criteria. Hand movement or arrival without evidence that the target was transported is insufficient. Do not additionally require placement, release, or closure unless those are explicitly part of this node's criteria.

- Place and release: Confirm the target is inside/on the correct destination. If release is required, it can be established by visible separation, independent resting at the destination, or a sequence showing the target entering the destination and the hand withdrawing without it. The target need not remain visible inside an opaque container when the sequence establishes placement. Disappearance without evidence of entry is insufficient.

- Close: Confirm the required closed endpoint. Movement toward closure, a reduced opening, or an obscured opening alone does not establish that endpoint. Do not require a tighter closure than the success criteria specify.
