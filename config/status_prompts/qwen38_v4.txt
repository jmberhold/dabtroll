STATUS RULES

- complete: The required success outcome is established by a clear visible state or consistent temporal evidence. Brief occlusion does not prevent completion when the surrounding observations establish the required outcome. Occlusion, disappearance, or expected motion alone does not establish completion. - running: The required outcome remains unmet, or the available evidence cannot distinguish completion from partial execution. Do not return running solely because fingers or the target are temporarily hidden when the visible sequence otherwise establishes completion.

- failure: Clear evidence establishes an unrecoverable failure or a precondition violation explicitly defined as terminal. Lack of visible completion alone is not failure.
