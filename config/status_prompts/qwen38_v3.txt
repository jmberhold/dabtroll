STATUS RULES

Choose exactly one status:

- complete: The required success outcome is established by a clear visible state or consistent temporal evidence. Brief occlusion does not prevent completion when the surrounding observations establish the required outcome. Occlusion, disappearance, or expected motion alone does not establish completion. Once the required outcome has been clearly demonstrated, do not demand another confirmation merely because a later view is obscured.

- running: The required outcome remains unmet, or the available evidence cannot distinguish completion from partial execution. Do not return running solely because fingers or the target are temporarily hidden when the visible sequence otherwise establishes completion.

- failure: Clear evidence establishes an unrecoverable failure or a precondition violation explicitly defined as terminal. Lack of visible completion alone is not failure.

ACTION EXAMPLES

- Grasp and lift: Confirm the target is visibly held and lifted, or that it remains coupled to the hand as it moves clear of its supporting surface. Visible fingers are not required when the motion establishes attachment and lift.

- Place and release: Confirm the target is inside/on the destination.

- Close: Confirm item appears in closed configuration.

OUTPUT CONSISTENCY

- criteria_met: Include supported criterion parts.
- criteria_missing: Include every remaining mandatory part; distinguish "not achieved" from "not visible/unverified".
- Do not list the same criterion in both arrays.
- if complete criteria_missing should be empty.
- notes should identify what establishes completion or which decisive requirement remains unmet/unverified. Reference frame numbers when available.
- Evaluate this node only; do not infer completion of later nodes.

HISTORY AND PROGRESS

- progress_score is an auxiliary description of visible change, not completion probability or permission to advance.
- Use 0.50 for no demonstrated change or insufficient comparison, above 0.50 for advancement, and below 0.50 for regression.
- Use 0.00 for failure and 1.00 for completion
- Scores may increase or decrease with evidence. Do not force smooth or monotonic scores.
