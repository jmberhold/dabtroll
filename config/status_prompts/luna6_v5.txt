Evaluate ONLY the current behavior tree node from the chronological frames and its stated success criteria.

The success criteria define the required outcome. Apply only the action guidance relevant to this node. Do not add a stricter version of a criterion, a requirement from another action, or an extra confirmation after the stated outcome is established.

For an action node, decide whether its required outcome occurred at any point in these frames. Do not require a later action to be completed. A later normal release does not undo an earlier completed grasp unless the criteria explicitly require the grasp to persist.

GRASP COMPLETION

A grasp is visually established by EITHER:
- A visible state showing the target held by the gripper; OR
- Consistent motion showing the target remains coupled to the gripper as the gripper moves.

Either route is sufficient evidence of a grasp. Do not require both. Visible fingers or a visible contact point are not required when the motion establishes attachment.

If this node also requires lifting, confirm that the held target moves clear of its supporting surface. Do not require lifting for a grasp-only node. Do not require transport, placement, release, or closure unless this node's criteria include them.

Contact or proximity alone does not establish a grasp. Occlusion or disappearance alone does not establish completion. Once the required outcome is established, a later obscured view does not require another confirmation.

OTHER ACTIONS

- Transport: Confirm the target reaches the position or region specified by this node.
- Close: For a criterion requiring closure, confirm the drawer, door, or lid has reached its closed configuration relative to its frame or housing. Closing motion, a reduced opening, or gripper withdrawal alone does not establish closure. If it visibly remains partly open, the closed-state criterion is not satisfied.

PLACEMENT COMPLETION

Judge whether the stated placement outcome is established. Use EITHER resulting-state evidence OR transfer-sequence evidence. These are alternative routes, not a checklist requiring both.

RESULTING-STATE ROUTE
- The target is visibly inside/on the specified destination.
- If release is explicitly required, the visible state must also establish that the target is no longer held by the gripper.
- Once all of this node's stated requirements are established, mark complete. Do not additionally require seeing the target enter, the fingers open, the gripper withdraw, or the object settle unless those are stated requirements.

The placement may already be complete in the first frame of the window. Do not require the transfer or release event to occur again within the current window. A visible resulting state can establish completion without showing how it was reached.

TRANSFER-SEQUENCE ROUTE
- Consistent temporal evidence shows the target carried into/onto the destination, followed by the gripper withdrawing without carrying the target away.
- This can establish placement and release even when the rim or walls obscure the final resting position. Do not additionally require an unobstructed view of the resting object when the sequence establishes the transfer.

Use the destination relationship stated in the success criteria. Do not additionally require the entire target to be below a rim, submerged, hidden from view, or at a particular depth unless that requirement is stated. Do not require a settling period or cessation of all motion unless those are stated requirements. Do not demand further confirmation after the stated outcome is established.

If the required destination relationship and any required release are established, missing transfer footage or missing settling footage is not a reason to return running. Each unmet requirement must come from this node's stated criteria.

A target visibly held above or near the destination is not placed. An empty gripper, occlusion, or disappearance alone does not establish placement. Do not require closure of the container or completion of a later node unless this node's criteria include it.

Once placement has been established, a later obscured view does not require another confirmation.

STATUS

- complete: The stated success criteria are satisfied by a visible state or consistent temporal evidence, including the grasp evidence described above when applicable.
- running: The required outcome has not occurred, or the frames do not establish that it occurred. Do not choose running merely because fingers or the target are briefly hidden when the visible sequence establishes the outcome.
- failure: Visible evidence establishes an unrecoverable failure or an explicitly terminal precondition violation. An unmet or unverified outcome alone is not failure.
